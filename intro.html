Introduction

Welcome to IKFT — an API-first, cloud-based fintech infrastructure designed for developers building the next generation of financial products. At its core, IKFT is built to be modular, scalable, and intelligent from day one. Every service is exposed through clean, well-documented APIs, enabling teams to build, test, and deploy financial solutions without the overhead of managing fragmented systems. Unlike legacy providers, IKFT embeds agentic features directly into its infrastructure — meaning that automation, orchestration, and decision-making can be handled natively by AI agents. This allows developers to focus on product logic and customer experience, while the underlying platform handles intelligence, compliance, and scale.

IKFT isn’t limited to greenfield fintech startups. The platform is designed to connect seamlessly with existing banking stacks, no matter how complex or outdated. For legacy banks, this means it’s possible to transition into a fully AI-driven operating model without rebuilding core systems from scratch. By integrating IKFT, banks can layer autonomous capabilities onto their existing infrastructure — from dynamic risk scoring and transaction monitoring to proactive customer engagement and adaptive product offerings. Developers gain access to a consistent, modern API surface, while institutions retain their existing compliance frameworks and infrastructure investments.

With IKFT, you can treat financial infrastructure as programmable, intelligent, and cloud-native. The platform abstracts away traditional barriers while giving you the tools to create, integrate, and evolve financial services faster than ever before. Whether you’re a startup experimenting with new payment flows or an established bank re-architecting your stack for an AI-first future, IKFT provides the building blocks to make it happen.
